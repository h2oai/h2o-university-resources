H2O.ai Solution Engineer Enablement

A dedicated solution engineering certification quiz, separate from the existing Partner certification at ClassMarker. 30 questions mapped onto the six modules above, weighted to how much of the track each one carries — a mix of concept checks and customer-scenario judgment. Both the question order and the answer options are randomized on every attempt, so no two sittings look the same:

3 questions · Module 1
The H2O.ai platform — the end-to-end AI lifecycle, the three paradigms of AI, and data traceability for governance and audit
3 questions · Module 2
H2O.ai Managed Cloud — what the managed environment delivers, predictive and generative side by side, and the admin surface IT asks about: users, roles, compute
6 questions · Module 3

9 questions · Module 4
Generative AI — RAG and why enterprises need it, h2oGPTe collections, guardrails, hybrid retrieval and 50+ ingestion formats, plus LoRA and LLM Studio fine-tuning
5 questions · Module 5
AI agents — chains vs. agents, routing as the decision engine, MCP for multi-agent orchestration, the Telco churn demo, and placing MLOps against a real prospect pain
4 questions · Module 6
Specialized tools — Label Genie annotation and zero-shot labeling, Hydrogen Torch for unstructured deep learning, and H2O Wave for AI-powered apps
